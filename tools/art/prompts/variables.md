# 提示词变量表

## 目标

把常用 prompt 字段固定成变量，方便后续批量生成同一角色的多套资源。

## 角色变量

- `{character}`
  - yellow basketball chick mascot
- `{body_shape}`
  - round body
- `{beak_color}`
  - orange beak
- `{accessory}`
  - sporty headband
- `{style}`
  - clean mobile game sprite style
- `{outline}`
  - bold clean outline

## 表情变量

- `{emotion}`
  - calm
  - happy
  - hyped
  - broken
- `{mood}`
  - neutral
  - cheerful
  - overexcited
  - defeated

## 动作变量

- `{pose}`
  - idle pose
  - holding a basketball
  - charging a basketball shot
  - releasing a basketball shot
  - celebrating a successful shot
  - failed shot reaction

## 背景变量

- `{scene}`
  - home screen
  - basketball court
  - result screen
- `{palette}`
  - bright sporty colors
  - cheerful blue and orange palette
  - warm sunset court palette

## 质量变量

- `{quality}`
  - polished
  - clean readable
  - high clarity
- `{background_mode}`
  - transparent background
  - simple background
  - no text

## 负面变量

- `{negative}`
  - realistic feathers, complex lighting, text watermark, extra limbs, horror expression
